Welcome to Manchester United

Manchester United is one of the most famous football clubs in the world. The club was founded in 1878 and plays its home matches at Old Trafford in Manchester, England. The team plays in red shirts and is known as the Red Devils.

Old Trafford - The Theatre of Dreams

Manchester United has won many trophies including the Premier League, FA Cup, and UEFA Champions League. Millions of fans support the club from every country in the world.
