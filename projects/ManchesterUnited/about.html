About Manchester United FC

Manchester United Football Club is a professional football club based in Old Trafford, Greater Manchester, England. The club plays in the Premier League, which is the top level of English football.

  • Full Name: Manchester United Football Club
  • Nickname: The Red Devils
  • Founded: 1878
  • Stadium: Old Trafford
  • Stadium Capacity: 74,310
  • Rival Clubs: Manchester City, Liverpool, Leeds United

About Old Trafford

Old Trafford is the home of Manchester United. It opened in 1910 and is nicknamed the Theatre of Dreams. The stadium has four stands: Sir Alex Ferguson Stand, East Stand, Sir Bobby Charlton Stand, and Stretford End. The Stretford End is where the loudest fans sit.
