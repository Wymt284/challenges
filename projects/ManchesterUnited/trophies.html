Premier League Titles (20)

Manchester United has won the English top division 20 times:

  • 1907-08, 1910-11, 1951-52, 1955-56, 1956-57, 1964-65, 1966-67
  • 1992-93, 1993-94, 1995-96, 1996-97, 1998-99, 1999-2000, 2000-01
  • 2002-03, 2006-07, 2007-08, 2008-09, 2010-11, 2012-13

Sir Alex Ferguson led the club to 13 Premier League titles between 1993 and 2013.

The Treble (1998-99)

In the 1998-99 season Manchester United won the Premier League, the FA Cup, and the UEFA Champions League in the same season. No English club had done this before. It is the greatest season in the club's history.

Premier League + FA Cup + Champions League = The Treble
