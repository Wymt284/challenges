Club History

Over 140 years of triumph, tragedy, and glory

Manchester United has a long and proud history. The club has seen great triumphs and sad moments, but it always fights back. Here are the most important events in our story.

From Newton Heath to the Theatre of Dreams

How It All Started

The club was founded in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR Football Club by workers at the Lancashire and Yorkshire Railway depot. In 1902 the club changed its name to Manchester United. In 1910 the team moved to Old Trafford, which is still our home today.

Important Moments in Time

1908 - First League Title

Manchester United won the Football League First Division for the first time in 1908. They also won the FA Cup in 1909.

1945 - Sir Matt Busby Arrives

Matt Busby became manager and built a young team called the Busby Babes. They won league titles in 1956 and 1957.

1958 - Munich Air Disaster

On 6 February 1958, the plane carrying the team crashed in Munich. Eight players and three club staff lost their lives. The club remembered them with pride and rebuilt the team.

1968 - First European Cup

Ten years after Munich, Manchester United became the first English club to win the European Cup. They beat Benfica 4-1 in the final at Wembley.

1986 - Sir Alex Ferguson

Alex Ferguson became manager in November 1986. He went on to become the most successful manager in British football history.

1999 - The Treble

In 1999 Manchester United won the Premier League, FA Cup, and Champions League in the same season. Teddy Sheringham and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer scored late goals to beat Bayern Munich in the final.

2008 - Champions in Moscow

United won the Champions League again in 2008. Cristiano Ronaldo and the team lifted the trophy in Moscow after a penalty shootout.

2013 - Ferguson Retires

Sir Alex Ferguson retired after winning his 13th Premier League title. Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, and many legends played under him.

Legendary Players

  • George Best - Skilful winger of the 1960s
  • Bobby Charlton - Munich survivor and World Cup winner
  • Eric Cantona - King Eric, key player of the 1990s
  • Ryan Giggs - Most appearances for the club
  • Cristiano Ronaldo - Goal machine, Ballon d'Or winner
  • Wayne Rooney - Club's all-time top scorer
